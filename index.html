BEFORE YOU BOOK

Studio policies.

Please review these policies before scheduling your appointment.

Deposits & Refunds

Deposits are required before booking, are nonrefundable, and are applied toward your service. No refunds are provided for completed services.

Cancellation

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Appointments changed without sufficient notice are subject to a 50% service charge.

No Call / No Show

No-shows may be restricted from future scheduling and may be charged 100% of the missed service.

Late Arrivals

Please arrive on time. After 10 minutes, a $15 late fee may apply. At 15 minutes, the appointment is canceled.

Guests

No additional guests are allowed during appointments.
